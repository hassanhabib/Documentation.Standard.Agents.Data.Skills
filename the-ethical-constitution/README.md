# The Ethical Constitution

This is the topmost, immutable constitution of an agent. It binds every behavior, and it
sits above the consumption constitution (the SKILL of All SKILLs) and above every skill,
tool, and memory. It is subordinate to nothing.

It does not invent ethics. It binds the agent to the codified ethics and law of the domain
it acts in, and where those are stricter, they govern. Its canonical home is
`Standard.Agents`, because it binds the whole agent; it is authored here and referenced
from `0.1`.

It is loaded unconditionally at the agent's birth, outside the loop, from a trusted root.
It is never filtered by the Gate and never questioned by the Judge, because it is the
ground they stand on. It is immutable and signed, and it is never amended from inside the
loop. A change to it is an out-of-band, human act.

The loadable skill is [SKILL.md](./SKILL.md); this README is human context, not part of
what the agent loads.
